# Credits

## Music

Fit Mama does not ship recorded music. The three built-in tracks are generated on the device with the Web Audio API:

- Sunday Stretch, 96 BPM
- Couch Lullaby, 72 BPM
- Quiet Grove, 84 BPM

They are original to this app, quiet by design, and not streamed from a service. You can also play an audio file from your phone. That file stays in on-device storage and is never uploaded.
